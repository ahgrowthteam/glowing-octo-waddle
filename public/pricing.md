# ExamGhost Pricing

Last updated: 2026-11-19

ExamGhost is an AI Chrome extension by FoundryOne LLC that answers quiz and homework questions on the page. It has a free tier and three paid plans.

| Plan | Price | Effective monthly cost | Billing | Notes |
|------|-------|------------------------|---------|-------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | None | 5 solves a day, no card required |
| Monthly | $19 | $19.00 | Billed every month | 14-day money-back guarantee |
| Yearly | $99 | about $8.25 | Billed once a year | 14-day money-back guarantee |
| Lifetime | $199 | n/a | One-time payment | 14-day money-back guarantee |

- Money-back guarantee: 14 days on paid plans.
- Prices are in US dollars and were current as of 2026-11-19. Check https://www.examghost.com for the latest prices.
- Terms: https://www.examghost.com/terms
- Questions: support@examghost.com

## Features

- Double-click a question to solve it (multiple choice, multi-select, dropdown, short answer)
- Three solver modes, switched with Alt+1 / Alt+2 / Alt+3: quick, highlight, explain
- Alt+S screenshot snip for graphs, diagrams and image questions
- Scan the whole page
- Equation and LaTeX reading
- Tuned prompts for 13 subjects

The free tier is limited to 5 solves a day. Paid plans remove the daily limit, and that includes screenshot (Alt+S) solves. See the site for current plan details.

## Note

Answers come from a Gemini-based AI model and can be wrong. Instructors may restrict AI tools on graded work; follow your school's academic integrity policy.
