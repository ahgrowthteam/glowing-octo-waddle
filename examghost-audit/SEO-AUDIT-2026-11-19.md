# ExamGhost SEO / spam-risk audit — 2026-11-19

Scope: local site (localhost:3005, production domain https://www.examghost.com). Method: crawl of every sitemap URL plus all
`*-vs-examghost` routes (102 pages), measuring duplicate text (6-word shingle overlap), unique-content ratio, titles/metas,
canonicals, robots meta, sitemap/index consistency, schema, internal links. Performance (Core Web Vitals) was NOT measured
(dev server only). The generic multi-agent `seo-audit` fan-out was not run; this was a focused audit of the owner's question:
"could Google / other engines / AI engines treat the site as spam?"

## Verdict

Yes, there was real risk, concentrated in two places, now mitigated.

| Group | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy comparison pages (no verified data), 36 | 34 indexable, 327-353 words, 58% shared text, one identical pair, median 25% unique | **0 indexable** (noindex,follow; out of sitemap, llms.txt and /compare) |
| Platform pages, 20 | 20 indexable, median 19% unique, 62% shared (doorway-page shape) | **8 indexable** (the keyword-backed ones) with unique platform context; 12 noindex until enriched. 41% shared, 39% unique |
| Verified comparison pages, 35 | 45% shared, median 528 words | 42% shared, median 602 words, plus a computed per-competitor "at a glance" paragraph |
| Sitemap | 99 URLs | 52 URLs, all indexable, none noindex |

Other fixes: `/features` title 72 -> 53 chars and meta 177 -> 158; platform metas all 90-160; two comparison pages that had the
same title are now distinct (CanvasQuiz / CanvasQuizSolve); `/classlogy-vs-examghost` (duplicate typo page) now a permanent
redirect; two orphaned, detection-framed blog posts noindexed and removed from the sitemap; homepage JSON-LD no longer lists
404 social profiles or a search action that doesn't exist; `/llms.txt` is generated so it can't go stale.

## Still open (not fixed here)

1. **Privacy and Terms are indexable and still contain wording about evading instructor detection** and a Claude/ChatGPT/DeepSeek
   workspace the extension doesn't have (privacy.tsx ~L107/115; terms.tsx ~L113/115/122/138/150). Rewrite with legal review.
2. **"Unlimited solves"** on paid plans is copied from the pricing page; the extension code shows a 50-message quota. Substantiate
   before using it in ads or schema.
3. **12 noindex platform pages** (Cengage, Macmillan, WileyPlus, Hawkes, Knewton, Edgenuity, DeltaMath, WeBWorK, Gradescope, Top Hat,
   Sakai, zyBooks): add genuinely unique content (`blurb` + more), then set `indexable: true` in `src/data/platforms.ts`.
4. **36 legacy comparison pages**: research each (see research/gen-facts.py), add facts; they become indexable automatically.
5. **Blog posts** are noindexed, not deleted. Decide whether to keep, rewrite or remove.
6. **Core Web Vitals / images** not measured; run Lighthouse against a production build.
7. **Production headers:** `build:vercel` copies `next.config.vercel.mjs` over `next.config.mjs`, so Vercel has no security/CSP headers;
   a Cloudflare move must carry `media-src` / `frame-src` (youtube-nocookie.com) allowances.
8. Vendor-supplied numbers (ratings, user counts) are intentionally not published; keep it that way.
